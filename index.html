I am an undergraduate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (B.S. in Computer Science, minor in Statistics, expected May 2027), advised by Prof. Hao Peng in the Alta Research Group. I have also been fortunate to work with Prof. Angela Lyons and Prof. Aiman Soliman at NCSA, with Xiaocong Yang in the AI Interpretability Group at Illinois, and with Vasu Sharma at Pocket FM.

My research asks how LLM agents reason and why they act as they do. To this end, I work on:

  • Evaluating agent reasoning against expert judgment, step by step, with metrics that measure what they claim to [More Context Is Not Validation, clinical-sim].
  • Whether the reasoning an agent writes down is what actually drives its behavior, and what a probe that reads it is really reading [ReAct Phase Probes].
  • Training agents that reason like experts: environments that score the process, not only the answer, so that agents can be trained with RL rather than only evaluated [clinical-sim, context hand-off in coding agents].

I also contribute bug fixes upstream to the open-source tools I use for research (TransformerLens, marimo, OpenClaw, pylint, PyTorch, vLLM).

I graduate in May 2027 and am applying to graduate programs in computer science. If you work on agent evaluation or reasoning, please feel free to reach out!

🔧 Projects

  • clinical-sim: an RL environment for sequential clinical diagnosis. The agent questions, examines and orders tests before committing to a diagnosis, and every step is scored against what physicians were thinking at that moment. Alta Research Group, 2026–present.
  • Phantom: an autonomous web penetration-testing agent. A Playwright crawler maps the attack surface and a ReAct loop plans and executes multi-step exploit chains, returning CVSS-scored findings. HackIllinois 2026; selected for Y Combinator's Startup School 2026. [Demo]
  • Adaptation: the localization component of Sherpa, Pocket FM's AI writing platform (launched Sept 2026). It rewrites full scripts into another language and adapts names, settings and cultural references.
  • Blockbuster Engine: a launch-outcome predictor for Pocket FM combining LoRA-tuned LLMs with gradient-boosted trees and neural networks (80% accuracy vs. a 34% baseline), 2025.
  • BeClear: an AI college-admissions advising agent built on RAG with LoRA-fine-tuned models; 200+ users across three partner schools; acquired in 2025.
  • ReAct Interp: a one-week GPT-2 probing sprint that became the preregistered Gemma-2 study above. [Code]
